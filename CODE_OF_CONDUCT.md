# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity. Contributors, maintainers and users all share responsibility for that.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent; disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give constructive, specific feedback, and accept it graciously.
- Help newcomers; everyone was new once.
- Respect people's privacy. Don't share someone's chats, ledgers or dossiers without permission, including in issues and bug reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project) and to private messages related to the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems to **ansh.sharma@live.com** or privately to a maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially and promptly. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behavior violates this code, in proportion to the harm caused.
